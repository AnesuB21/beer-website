About the Beer

Our Brown IPA is a light and refreshing 5.3% ABV beer, combining a distinctly hoppy character with fruity and tropical aromas and flavours. Its distinct brown colour comes from darker malts, which contribute subtle chocolate and toffee undertones without overpowering the beer’s lighter body. The tropical hop character complements the malt sweetness and balances the bitterness, creating a smooth, refreshing beer that is easy to enjoy.

Beer History

The bontebok was chosen as an inspiration for our label not only because it is endemic to South Africa but also because of its meaningful conservation story and its connection to our brewery's location. In 1931, seven bonteboks were translocated from Bredasdorp National Park and sent to Thornkloof Ranch in Grahamstown, now Makhanda. Of those seven antelope, five were successfully introduced, creating a new, distinct breeding pool that supported their population levels and a successful conservation story. Our beer label aims to highlight the beauty of South African wildlife and the importance of conserving our species. As such, 5% of our profits go to South African conservation agencies, such as Wildlife ACT, that focus on conserving critically endangered species.
